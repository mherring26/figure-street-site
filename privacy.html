Privacy policy

Figure Street, by Bruin Labs LLC. Effective October 23, 2026.

In short: Figure Street does not collect your child's personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no chat. The game works offline. The only thing that ever goes over the internet is a check of the one purchase.

What stays on the device

Your child's progress (their shopkeeper's look and name, shops, coins, decor, levels and answers) is saved only on the device. We cannot see it, and it is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

The skill report a grown-up sees is made on the device from that same saved progress. It is shared only if a grown-up chooses Share and sends the picture themselves.

The one purchase

The full game is a single purchase made through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see card or account details.

To know whether the purchase was made, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. When the app opens or a grown-up buys or restores, RevenueCat checks the purchase with Apple under a random ID that the app creates, which is not tied to your name, email, Apple Account or device. RevenueCat keeps a record of the purchase for that random ID so it can be restored. It is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or email collection.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
  • No chat, no messages between players, no links out of the app for children.
  • We do not sell or share any information.

Children

Figure Street is made for children. It follows Apple's Kids Category rules and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act: we do not collect personal information from children. Purchases, settings, the skill report and any link out of the app are behind a question only a grown-up can answer.

Read aloud

Orders are read aloud with the voice built into the device. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions: contact@bruinlabsllc.com.
